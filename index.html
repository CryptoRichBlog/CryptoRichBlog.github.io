FAQ

Questions delegators ask.

If I delegate to CRICH, does my ADA leave my wallet?

No. Delegating hands CRICH the job of staking, which means producing blocks for Cardano’s proof of stake. It does not hand over your ADA. Your ADA stays in your own wallet, the operator can never touch it, and you can send or sell it at any time with no lock-up. Cardano has no slashing, so a server problem can never cost you ADA. Think of delegating as saying “I back this pool.”

If I switch from another pool, do my rewards stop?

No, rewards keep flowing without a gap. Cardano takes a snapshot of every delegation at each epoch boundary and pays rewards from snapshots taken a few epochs earlier. So change pools in your wallet’s staking menu instead of undelegating first: your old pool pays for the epoch you switch in and the next one, and CRICH pays from the third epoch on.

Does a smaller pool earn less?

Not per ADA. When every pool runs normally, the Cardano protocol pays the same rate per ADA before fees, roughly 2% a year as of October 2026. Each delegated ADA is one ticket in the block lottery, so over a year 1 ADA earns the same in a 10,000 ADA pool as in a 70 million ADA pool. Two honest footnotes: a small pool’s rewards arrive in bumps rather than every epoch, and every pool deducts the protocol’s fixed 170 ADA minimum fee in each epoch it makes a block, which weighs more while a pool is small. CRICH charges only that minimum, with a 0% margin. One delegation, two rewards. Delegating to CRICH earns ADA today, and Midnight, Cardano’s privacy partner chain, plans to open block production to Cardano pools in Q1 2027 and pay NIGHT to the pools and delegators who take part. So the same delegation would earn NIGHT on top, and CRICH intends to join as soon as it opens. NIGHT amounts aren’t published yet, so the 2% above is ADA only.

Will CRICH ever ask for my recovery phrase?

Never. CRICH never asks for your recovery phrase or a wallet connection, and never messages you first. Anyone who asks in our name is a scammer.

I see several pools with the same ticker.

Tickers are not unique, and copycat pools have existed. Always check the pool ID shown on this page.

When do rewards start?

About 15 to 20 days after your first delegation, then every 5 days. Cardano takes a snapshot of delegations at each epoch boundary and pays from older snapshots.

Do I need to delegate to a DRep?

Yes, to withdraw your rewards. DReps (delegated representatives) vote on Cardano’s on-chain governance for the ADA delegated to them, a bit like members of parliament. Since the Plomin upgrade in January 2025, rewards keep building up without one, but withdrawing them needs a DRep delegation: pick any DRep or choose “Abstain”. CRICH plans to register as a DRep too, and will ask delegators in the KakaoTalk open chat how to vote.

Some epochs show zero blocks. Is something wrong?

No, that is normal for a small pool. Block slots are a lottery in which each delegated ADA is one ticket. At about 620,000 ADA the pool expects 0.6 blocks per epoch, so roughly every other epoch has none. Over a year it evens out.

Is the fee really zero?

Not quite, and we say so plainly. Every Cardano pool must charge a fixed fee of at least 170 ADA per epoch. CRICH charges exactly that minimum with a 0% margin, so nothing is added on top.

Isn't a bigger pool always better?

Only up to the saturation point, about 77.8M ADA in October 2026. Above it, rewards per ADA fall. Below it, every block is worth the same.

Will I earn NIGHT by delegating here?

That is the plan, and the timeline is now on Midnight’s official roadmap: block producers start earning NIGHT in Q4 2026, then in Q1 2027 Cardano stake pool operators can join Midnight as block producers and NIGHT staking opens. Midnight’s tokenomics paper (v1.81, May 2026) says NIGHT block rewards go to participating pools and to the delegators whose ADA stake lets them produce blocks, so your ADA delegation itself is what earns NIGHT; you don’t need to hold NIGHT. CRICH, run by a NightForce member, plans to register the day it opens and will post the exact steps on the blog as soon as Midnight publishes them. Amounts aren’t published yet, so this is a plan, not a promise.
